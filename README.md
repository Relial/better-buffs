## Overview

Makes feeding guild poogies a 100% chance to give you a buff.

Preserves guild poogie buff and tore poogie held item through crashes/disconnects/relogs for 12 hours after taking them, and guild food buff for its 90min duration.

## Requirements

[Bunny Manager](https://github.com/Relial/bunny-manager)

## Usage

Extract in the MHFZ game folder.

Configure in the manager's plugin menu.
